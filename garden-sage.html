Garden Sage

A garden and grow-management assistant — like having a master gardener in your pocket.

Point your camera at a plant, and Garden Sage is built to tell you what it needs and when — no account setup, no forms, no menus to learn first.

Most plant apps make you do the work: identify the species, fill in a profile, set your own reminders. Garden Sage is being designed to start from a photo and do the figuring out for you, then keep track of the rest so your garden doesn't depend on your memory.

What it's being built to do

  • Snap and identify. A photo is enough to recognise the plant and start a care plan.
  • Care guidance. Light, soil, and the common mistakes for that particular plant.
  • Lifecycle planning. What to expect through the seasons, and what to do next.
  • Watering & feeding schedules. Sensible reminders, set for you, easy to adjust.

Where it stands

In active development — the photo identification and scheduling are planned features, not live yet. We're building and testing them inside the studio. There's no public release, and we won't claim a feature works until it does. When there's something to try, you'll find it here first.
