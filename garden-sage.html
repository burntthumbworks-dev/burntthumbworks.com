Garden Sage

A straightforward garden planner to keep track of your beds, planting timing, and what's bothering your crops—whether you're tending a few pots on the porch or a backyard vegetable patch.

Open Live Slice Keep me posted How it works

Garden Sage is built to take the guesswork out of the growing season—without burying you in charts or clutter.

Most gardeners just want a tidy place to remember what went into which bed, when last frost actually clears, and what to check when a leaf starts yellowing or curling. Whether you've got three pots on a balcony or a dozen raised beds, everything stays simple and readable.

What it helps you keep track of

  • Beds, pots, and trays. Keep each growing spot organized with what's planted and how far along it is.
  • Planting timing. Clear frost windows and seasonal sowing reminders so seeds go out at the right week.
  • Crop troubleshooting. Plain-English checks for common pests, watering trouble, and soil or leaf symptoms.
  • Notes across the season. Jot down what thrived, what struggled, and what you'd change next spring.

Where it stands

You can open the live slice today to try out the bed planner, planting windows, and crop checks. The fuller photo-note and Android build are still taking shape—if you want a heads-up when they're ready, drop us a note.
