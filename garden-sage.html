Garden Sage

A public-safe preview of a source-backed master gardener app in development for observations, planning, task timing, and season records.

Garden Sage is being built as a master gardener in your pocket: an assistant for turning observations into practical next steps, not just another plant-photo identifier.

This page is a sanitized web demo of the intended user loop. It shows the public concept: record what you see, review a cautious draft, turn the decision into a task, and keep a season history. Private build details, internal workflows, and future packaging remain out of view.

What the demo shows

  • Observation intake. Capture a plant note, photo, location label, and timing context.
  • Review-first drafts. Draft guidance is presented as something to verify, not as automatic authority.
  • Task conversion. Decisions become practical work items with timing and follow-up.
  • Season records. Notes and outcomes are meant to build a useful record over time.

Where it stands

Garden Sage is in early development. The web demo below is public-facing concept work, not a live app connection. There is no public release, no downloadable app yet, no live AI service connected here, and no live customer data collection through this page.
