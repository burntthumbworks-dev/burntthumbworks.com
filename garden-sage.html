Garden Sage

A garden and grow companion for people who actually need to remember what they did, when they did it, and what should happen next. Logs, notes, schedules, reminders, and care decisions in one place — not just pretty plant pictures.

← Back to projects

What it does

Built for people who grow things

If you've ever forgotten when you last fed something, or what you planted where, this is for that. Keep the record, get the nudge, make the call.

Plant logs

Track what you're growing and what you've done to it — by plant, bed, or batch. The history you'll wish you'd kept.

Notes

Quick notes that stay attached to the right plant, so observations don't vanish into a pile of photos.

Schedules & reminders

Watering and feeding schedules, and reminders for what's due — so care happens on time instead of from memory.

Care decisions

Practical guidance for the next step, grounded in what you actually logged — not generic plant-blog filler.
