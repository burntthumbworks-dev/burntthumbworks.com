Garden Sage

A garden and grow-management assistant in development, built to track plants, notes, schedules, reminders, and care decisions in one place — a practical record for people who grow things and need to remember what they did and what comes next.

Back to products

What it does

Keep the record, get the reminder, make the call

The essentials of grow management in one place — designed to be quick to update and easy to come back to.

Plant logs

Track what you are growing and what you have done, organized by plant, bed, or batch.

Notes

Observations kept with the right plant, so useful detail does not get lost over a season.

Schedules & reminders

Watering and feeding schedules, with reminders for what is due, so care happens on time.

Care decisions

Practical guidance for the next step, grounded in what has actually been recorded.

Who it is for

For people who actually grow things

Home gardeners and growers who want a dependable record and timely reminders, not just plant photos.

Status

Where it stands

In development

Record-keeping first

The current focus is the core that growers rely on: logs, notes, schedules, reminders, and care decisions.

Planned

Photo & AI assistance — planned

Photo-based identification and AI care suggestions are planned. They will be added when they are accurate enough to rely on, and are labeled as planned until then.
