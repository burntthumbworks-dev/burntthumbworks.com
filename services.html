Services

Practical packages for small operators who need usable websites, workflows, automations, records, and launch-ready prototypes.

Where we can help

Each engagement starts by turning the request into a clear scope, source list, deliverables list, and handoff plan.

Available

Website starter sprint

A clean small-business website route with core pages, responsive layout, basic metadata, sitemap, contact path, and launch checklist.

  • Best for: local services, portfolios, studios, and early product pages.
  • Output: source files, review branch, launch notes, and handoff checklist.
  • Approval gate: DNS, hosting changes, and public publish.
Available

Client intake to AI brief

A structured intake path that turns a client request into a practical project brief, source checklist, assumptions list, and first-pass task plan.

  • Best for: studios, consultants, trades, and repeat service workflows.
  • Output: intake fields, brief template, routing rules, and review checklist.
  • Approval gate: live forms, email automation, and client data handling.
Available

Workflow automation review

A practical review of repeated file, report, reminder, and handoff work, followed by small local scripts or templates where automation is worth it.

  • Best for: recurring admin tasks, reporting loops, and manual file routing.
  • Output: process map, risk notes, dry-run scripts, and operator instructions.
  • Approval gate: destructive actions, cloud access, and paid services.
Available

Records and reporting setup

Folder structure, naming rules, ledgers, receipt capture, mileage notes, and repeatable reports built for small-business operations.

  • Best for: sole proprietors, small studios, and operators preparing cleaner books.
  • Output: folder map, templates, review cadence, and status report format.
  • Approval gate: private financial records and tax/legal decisions.
Scoped

App or tool prototype sprint

A narrow prototype for a calculator, tracker, dashboard, internal tool, or product concept that needs real behavior before a full build.

  • Best for: testing product ideas, internal tools, and repeatable workflows.
  • Output: working demo, source notes, limitations list, and next-build estimate.
  • Approval gate: production architecture, user accounts, payments, and database changes.
Available

AI-assisted operations setup

A grounded AI workflow for intake, source folders, task packets, review points, reports, and session continuation across repeated production runs.

  • Best for: teams using AI but losing time to repeated setup and unclear context.
  • Output: operating folders, task packet pattern, report template, and restart rules.
  • Approval gate: private vaults, secrets, cloud tools, and customer data access.
