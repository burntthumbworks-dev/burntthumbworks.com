Pantry Panda

Practical kitchen inventory and meal planning — designed to simplify pantry management, reduce food waste, and make everyday cooking easier.

Try the Usable Slice → App Overview

Pantry Panda is an intuitive companion application designed to streamline kitchen organization and everyday meal planning.

The core concept is straightforward: an organized, clutter-free assistant that tracks pantry and refrigerator essentials, highlights items nearing expiration, and helps suggest meals based on ingredients you already have on hand. It represents the type of dependable, practical utility software Burnt Thumb Works is dedicated to building.

Interactive Web Slice Live

We work in the open, so we deployed an interactive web prototype you can test right now: organize kitchen stock across storage zones, run our deterministic recipe matching engine on your ingredients to see what you can cook today, simulate grocery hauls, and manage an interactive shopping list that restocks straight back to your pantry.

Follow Progress

You can follow the evolution of Pantry Panda and other studio utilities in the Studio Lab. If you have suggestions or specific features you would find valuable in a kitchen companion, we welcome your feedback.
