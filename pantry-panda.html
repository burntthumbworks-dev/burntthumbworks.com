Pantry Panda

Kitchen inventory and meal planning — shop smaller, cook smarter, waste less. An early app concept we're exploring out loud.

Pantry Panda is an early idea, not a finished product — and we're being upfront about that.

The thought behind it is simple: a friendly little helper for the everyday business of a kitchen — keeping track of what you've got on hand and taking some of the friction out of figuring out what to do with it. The kind of small, useful app the studio likes to make.

Where it stands

This one is still at the concept stage. We're sharing it here because we work in the open — the shape of it will change as we explore. There's nothing to download yet, and we won't pretend otherwise. When there's a prototype worth trying, this is where it'll show up.

Want to follow it?

The Lab is where prototypes and experiments get posted as they take shape. If Pantry Panda sounds like your kind of thing, keep an eye there — or get in touch and tell us what you'd want a kitchen helper to actually do.
