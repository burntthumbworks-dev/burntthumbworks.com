Pantry Panda

Keep tabs on your kitchen, fridge, and pantry, and see what you can make with what you already have.

Open Live Slice → App Entry

Figuring out dinner shouldn't start with ten minutes of staring into the cabinets wondering what's tucked behind the olive oil.

Pantry Panda gives you a clear look at what's sitting in your pantry, fridge, freezer, and spice rack. Match recipes to what's already on your shelves, cross off ingredients with a tap as you cook, and catch produce before it wilts.

What's ready to try

The live web slice lets you sort ingredients by kitchen spot, see recipes you can cook right now (or with one missing item), build a grocery list from what's running low, and restock your shelves when you get home from the store.

Two ways to run your kitchen

Switch anytime between shopping fresh every couple of days and keeping a well-stocked pantry for busy weeks.

🥖

Shop Like a European

Smaller, more frequent trips for fresh bread, greens, and seasonal produce—focused on what needs to be cooked in the next day or two so nothing spoils in the crisper drawer.

Best for: Quick weeknight meals, small kitchens, and fresh market runs.
🥫

Shop Like a Prepper

Keep steady staples on hand—rice, beans, canned tomatoes, cooking oils, and freezer meats—with gentle nudges when your backup shelves dip low and hearty batch-cooking ideas.

Best for: Bulk club hauls, family pantries, and fewer trips to the store.
