About the studio

An independent software studio dedicated to original web games, practical utility applications, and thoughtful design.

Burnt Thumb Works creates original, browser-playable games and dependable everyday applications — designed with care, built to last, and made accessible to everyone.

We believe great software should be immediately accessible. Our primary game releases — GrimmKart.io, Scarward, and The Hook and Skein — run seamlessly in modern web browsers with zero installation requirements or external account gates. Alongside our game development, we design practical companion applications like Garden Sage and Pantry Panda, sharing progress and technical previews through our Studio Lab.

Our Approach

We focus on deliberate craftsmanship, fast performance, and respect for user privacy and attention. Every project begins with an original idea and is developed into a polished, responsive experience that works smoothly across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

Heritage & Craft

The story behind our name and crest.

True understanding and craftsmanship come from direct experience, steady discipline, and dedicated hands-on creation.

The name Burnt Thumb Works is inspired by the ancient Celtic legend of the Salmon of Knowledge. In folklore, young Fionn was charged with tending the legendary salmon over an open hearth for the poet Finnegas. Touching the cooking fish to check its progress, he burned his thumb on the heat and instinctively drew it to his mouth, gaining wisdom and deep perspective from that direct contact.

For our studio, the legend represents a commitment to hands-on craftsmanship: building real systems, writing durable code, refining mechanics through play, and delivering software that speaks for itself through performance, reliability, and fun.

The Elements of the Crest

  • The Hearth & Skillet: An open fire in the foreground, representing hands-on creation, patience, and dedicated craft.
  • The Round Tower & Sanctuary: A classic stone haven beside the water, symbolizing quiet focus, stability, and enduring architecture.
  • The River: A flowing current representing folklore, continuous curiosity, and fresh ideas.
