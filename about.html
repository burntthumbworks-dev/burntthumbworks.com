About the studio

A small, independent studio making games, cool apps, and useful story-driven tools.

Burnt Thumb Works builds things worth playing with — original browser games, practical apps, and tools with a bit of story to them.

The studio works small and ships real things. GrimmKart.io, The Hook and Skein, and Scarward have playable builds in your browser today. Garden Sage and Pantry Panda are our original companion apps, and we post prototypes and vertical slices in the Lab as they're ready to show — we'd rather work in the open than disappear for a year and surprise you.

What we're about

Hand-made over mass-produced. Things that respect your time and attention. Small, original ideas carried all the way to something you can actually use or play.
