About the studio

A small, independent studio making games, cool apps, and useful story-driven tools.

Burnt Thumb Works builds things worth playing with — original browser games, practical apps, and tools with a bit of story to them.

The studio works small and ships real things. GrimmKart.io, The Hook and Skein, and Scarward have playable builds in your browser today. Garden Sage and Pantry Panda are our original companion apps, and we post prototypes and vertical slices in the Lab as they're ready to show — we'd rather work in the open than disappear for a year and surprise you.

Origin & Ethic

The story behind the name and crest.

Understanding doesn't come from sterile theory. It comes from direct contact, testing, and getting your hands burned by the actual work.

The name Burnt Thumb Works draws from the ancient Celtic legend of the Salmon of Knowledge. While cooking the fish over an open hearth for the master poet Finnegas, young Fionn pressed his thumb against a burning blister on its skin to protect the flesh. Instinctively putting his scorched thumb to his mouth to ease the burn, he tasted the oil of the salmon — and in that sudden, painful moment of direct contact, wisdom and insight became his.

For an independent studio, the burnt thumb is our operating doctrine: we don't build from detached spectator decks or speculative hype. We build by standing over the fire, writing the code, crafting the systems, and testing real software until it proves itself under your fingers.

The Elements of the Seal

  • The Hearth & Skillet: The cooking fire in the foreground — direct, tactile craft, patience, and honest labor.
  • The Round Tower & Sanctuary: A stone refuge standing beside the water — steady sanctuary, quiet focus, and durable architecture built to weather any season.
  • The River: The moving current carrying folklore, living curiosity, and continuous renewal.
