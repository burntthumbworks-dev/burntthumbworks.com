Engine Features

What the slice can do

The interactive slice runs 100% in your browser using local storage and deterministic matching logic. No live AI, no accounts, and zero cloud spend.

01 · Dual Philosophy

European vs. Prepper Modes

Switch anytime between "Shop Like a European" (daily neighborhood market stops, high turnover, 48h fresh meals) and "Shop Like a Prepper" (bulk wholesale cases, deep 30-day buffer tracking, low-reserve alerts).

02 · Track

Storage Zones & Buffers

Organize kitchen stock across Pantry, Fridge, Freezer, and Counter with freshness badges, count pickers, resilience meters, and instant search.

03 · Cook

Deterministic Matcher

Calculates "Ready to Make Now" (100% in-stock) vs "Almost There" recipes using canonical ingredients, weighted by your active shopping philosophy.

04 · Sourcing & Restock

Smart Store Dispatch & Intake

Send your missing ingredients directly to partner grocers (Instacart Fresh for European mode, Amazon Bulk / Costco for Prepper mode) and restock items back into inventory with one tap.
