How it will work

A simple, honest loop. Early builds keep photo drafting on your device — the app drafts what it sees, and you confirm before anything is saved.

  1. 1

    Photo

    Snap a plant. In early builds this stays on your device — no photo is sent off to a server.

  2. 2

    Draft

    Garden Sage drafts what it sees: a plant guess, a lifecycle stage, a care note.

  3. 3

    Verify

    You confirm or correct the draft. Nothing is treated as fact until you say so.
