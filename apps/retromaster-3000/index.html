RetroMaster 3000

Self-hosted 3D retro arcade dashboard for Windows + native Android / Fire TV / Google TV companion APK. Zero monthly fees.

Built for Living-Room Game Nights

Stop fighting clunky emulator menus and broken TV browsers. Run your own library locally on Windows 10/11 and play from any couch, phone, or browser.

1. 2001-Inspired 3D Console Hub

Chronological console carousel with automatic box-art and metadata fetching, historical blurbs, animated preview monitor, CRT scanline toggle, and switchable 90s Radical / Cyber 2001 / Laser Synth themes.

2. Built-In Free Legal Homebrew Store

Browse and 1-click install 1,100+ free, open-source Game Boy, GBC, GBA, and NES indie games directly from Homebrew Hub so you can test controllers and TV playback in seconds out of the box.

3. 4-Player Native Controller Bridge

Pre-mapped for Xbox, PlayStation, and Backbone controllers across all 4 players—including natural 2-button layouts for NES/GB, N64 Z-Trigger & C-Stick mappings, and full D-pad menu navigation on Fire TV & Google TV.

4. 12+ Consoles & Cross-Device Auto-Save

Play Atari 2600, NES, Master System, TurboGrafx-16, GB, GBC, GBA, SNES, N64, Nintendo DS, Sega Genesis, Game Gear, and PlayStation 1 natively in-app with automatic cloud-free saves every 25 seconds and household player profiles.

Important Software Notice

RetroMaster 3000 is 100% software only—zero ROMs, zero BIOS files, and zero copyrighted game assets are included. Bring your own legally owned backups or use the built-in legal Homebrew Store. Runs locally on Windows 10/11 at $0/month.
