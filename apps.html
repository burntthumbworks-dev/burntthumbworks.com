Garden Sage

An evidence-based horticulture companion backed by USDA and University Extension research. Select USDA Zones 5–9 to calculate frost dates, planting windows, and growth stages across beds, containers, and nursery trays. Features the Sage Advice diagnostic engine for diagnosing crop ailments and nutrient deficiencies, with a seasonal care scheduler and full citations.

Pantry Panda

A dual-philosophy kitchen inventory companion. Toggle between "Shop Like a European" (48-hour fresh turnover, daily market stops, near-zero food waste) and "Shop Like a Prepper" (bulk staple buffers, 30-day calorie cushions). Features shelf inventory tracking, a deterministic "What Can I Make?" recipe matcher with 1-tap stock deduction, and text receipt import.

Race 32 cursed tracks or battle across 10 closed arenas in a 3D folklore kart racer.
