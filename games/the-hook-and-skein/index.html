The Hook and Skein

6,500 years after the First Shearing. A mobile-first survival RPG of geography, debt, and consequence in Low Vale. You aren't the chosen one. Make your call and live with it.

Enter Low Vale All games

A world you read your way into.

An original Burnt Thumb Works narrative choice adventure about place, memory, debt, and consequence — grounded survival fantasy you read your way through.

The world does not begin with a chosen hero. It begins with work that still has to be done, people who still need feeding, roads that should not be followed, and a valley that remembers more than it should.

The player begins as someone ordinary enough to be missed and stubborn enough to matter. It's not about sprinting through a theme park of monsters. It's about learning what a place wants from you, what it has already taken, and whether you can survive long enough to change the pattern.

Low Vale, beneath the Black Spine.

In the shadow of the Black Spine, Low Vale sits close to the edge of things: ridges, hollows, drowned roads, old ruins, household obligations, and stories people tell because the truth is less useful than a warning.

It's grounded survival fantasy — closer to chores, debts, and dangerous old roads than to glory. The valley has its own logic, and local knowledge is a kind of armor.

What's in the build

  • Pick a character — the Hand, the Ledger-keeper, or the Quiet One
  • Mobile-first, tap-to-choose story cards through Low Vale
  • Visible state — Resolve, Burden, Memory, Supplies — that shifts with your choices
  • Consequence text and a short branching morning (old well, drowned ford, foot of the Black Spine)
  • Browser play with a local save and reset

What's being tested

The feel of the game: how it reads on mobile, how choices land, and whether consequence holds your attention. (The earlier typed-command build is kept as an archived systems prototype.)

Public Build · 100% Client-Side

This build runs local deterministic systems only. It makes zero third-party network calls, requires zero accounts, and runs instantly in your browser.

Where it's headed.

The Hook and Skein is being built as a mobile-first, browser-playable narrative choice adventure where choices, relationships, survival pressure, and local knowledge matter. The public demo is the first honest step — a short, tap-to-choose slice of Low Vale; richer systems and a deeper world come later, as they're ready.
