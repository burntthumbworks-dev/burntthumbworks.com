The Hook and Skein

A text-driven fantasy roleplaying world and narrative engine — written one careful thread at a time.

The Hook and Skein is a world you read your way into. No twitch reflexes, no flashing menus — just a place built from words, and the quiet systems holding it together underneath.

It's a fantasy roleplaying world driven by text. You make choices, follow threads, and watch a world respond. The aim is the feeling of a good campaign or a well-kept notebook: a place with its own logic, where what you did yesterday still matters today.

What we're after

Hand-written events and characters over endless procedural sludge. A narrative engine that keeps track of the world so the story stays coherent. Something that rewards attention rather than demanding speed — closer to reading by lamplight than grinding a feed.

Where it stands

In active development. The world, the systems, and the engine are being built and tested inside the studio. There's no public release yet, and we won't pretend otherwise. When there's something real to play, this is where it'll be announced.
