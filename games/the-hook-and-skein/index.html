A world that remembers your tread.

A narrative survival RPG about place, memory, debt, and the weight of what outlasts you.

The story does not begin with a chosen hero. It begins 6,500 years after the First Shearing—with work that still has to be done, neighbors who keep tallies, roads that should not be walked after dusk, and a valley that remembers more than it forgives.

You start as someone ordinary enough to be missed and stubborn enough to leave a trace. What you give up, who you stand beside, and what you turn away from stay etched into the valley long after the moment passes.

Low Vale, beneath the Black Spine.

In the shadow of the Black Spine, Low Vale sits close to the edge of things: ridges, hollows, drowned fords, old ruins, household obligations, and stories people tell because the truth is less useful than a warning.

It's grounded survival fantasy—closer to chores, debts, and muddy boots than to glory. Pay attention to the valley, and it tells you what it costs to survive it.
