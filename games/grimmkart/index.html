What GrimmKart is

Folklore, on wheels — built to grow.

GrimmKart is an original Burnt Thumb Works game prototype built around speed, strategy, tale-inspired chaos, and a huge expandable cast.

The game draws inspiration from classic folklore, fairy tales, and public-domain story traditions — featuring monsters, tricksters, heroes, mythical beasts, clever companions, and memorable figures from the margins of timeless tales.

GrimmKart is designed with an expandable universe in mind. A legendary figure from one tale, a cunning adversary from another, an enchanted animal, a helpful sprite, or a classic character with an iconic mechanic can all find a place in the roster — as racers, unlockable skins, companions, rivals, track bosses, and specialized vehicle themes.

All designs are original Burnt Thumb Works interpretations rooted in public-domain story traditions — celebrating the richness of folklore with fresh, modern gameplay.

The expandable roster

One cast, many roles.

Instead of a fixed number of characters or vehicles, GrimmKart is built around an expandable content model.

Racers & rivals

A large, growing cast of folklore-inspired drivers — heroes, villains, tricksters, and one-line side characters given a second life.

Karts

Vehicles with distinct feel and themes, from breadcrumb buggies to cursed coaches.

Companions & pets

Cursed creatures, strange helpers, and talking objects that ride along as collectible companions.

Skins & alternates

Alternate looks and variants earned through play as the cast expands.

Power sets

Item and ability themes drawn from each tale's logic, so characters play to their story.

Collectible identities

Lesser-known folklore figures become bosses, rivals, kart themes, or unlockable identities over time.

Current public prototype

Playable now, on desktop and mobile.

The current public prototype focuses on making GrimmKart playable in your browser and testing the core feel on desktop and mobile. Two modes ship: a Race Mode across cursed tracks and a Battle Arena Mode for free-drive item combat. No install, no account.

Storybook visual pass & 3D render pipeline

The live build wears a storybook design pass — candlelit menus, carved fairy-tale panels, flame-lit buttons, and self-hosted display type — paired with a quality-tiered "lit jewels in the dark" 3D render pipeline (ACES filmic tone mapping, selective bloom, vignette, and directional shadows on Medium/High, with a fast direct render on Low).

Mobile / Android touch controls

Includes a pointer-based mobile touch control system that keeps working when your thumb slides, supports steering and accelerating at the same time, sits clear of the Android browser bar, and can be moved, resized (S/M/L), and reset — saved locally on your device.
