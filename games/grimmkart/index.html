GrimmKart

Before the stories were scrubbed clean for nurseries, they were warnings. Pick a steed, take the crooked road, and battle friends across 32 cursed tracks and 10 arenas.

Play GrimmKart All games

Stray off the path — at your own risk.

The old tales weren't told to put anyone gently to sleep. They were road signs painted in soot to keep you on the cobbles.

GrimmKart puts you and your friends behind the reins in the darker corners of classic folklore—amid lantern-lit woods, crooked bridges, ash-choked kitchens, and crossroads where bargains go sour.

Drift tight corners to spark your boost, slipstream behind rivals, and throw 16 tale-born tricks—from breadcrumb traps and poisoned apples to wolf packs and iron shoes—across races and chaotic battle arenas.

Every rider, mount, and track draws from unvarnished public-domain story traditions, recast by Burnt Thumb Works into fast, unforgiving arcade combat.

Cast out of cautionary ink.

Tricksters, runaways, and creatures from the margins of the old books.

Racers & rivals

Folklore figures, stubborn villagers, and sly beasts who rarely survive to the final page without scars.

Cursed steeds & karts

From bone-rattling barrows and pumpkin carriages to stubborn farm livestock built for sharp turns.

16 wicked tricks

Hexes, snares, and blunt warnings plucked straight from story logic—meant to knock leaders back into the brambles.

32 crooked tracks

Grand Circuit roads weaving through cursed orchards, miller creeks, flooded cellars, and black pine ridges.

10 battle arenas

Closed courtyards and ruined keeps built for three-hit skirmishes where nobody gets a quiet lap.

Companions & alternates

Strange little helpers, alternate liveries, and unlocks earned out on the road.

Race, brawl, and settle scores.

Jump straight into Race Mode to test your lines on 32 tracks, or drop into Battle Arena Mode to trade blows with up to 12 racers on the field.

Solo, split-screen, or online

Practice alone against cunning rivals, share one screen with a friend beside you, or open a room code and bring friends along online.

Desktop & phone controls

Steer with keyboard, gamepad, or touch controls—and on phones, drag and resize the steer and throttle pads so they sit comfortably under your thumbs.
