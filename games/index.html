Games

Original games, free to play.

Burnt Thumb Works is building original games that start as playable prototypes and grow into full systems. Some are fast and chaotic. Some are slow, dangerous, and story-heavy. All of them are built around original worlds, expandable mechanics, and the belief that small studios can still make strange, memorable things.

Arcade kart game Playable Prototype

GrimmKart

A premium tale-inspired arcade game built for speed, strategy, unlocks, and a huge expandable roster of racers, rivals, karts, companions, pets, skins, and seasonal content. Free browser prototype — now with rebuilt mobile controls.

Story-driven survival RPG Public Demo

The Hook and Skein

A story-driven survival RPG about place, memory, debt, and consequence. Begin in Low Vale beneath the Black Spine and test the first safe public browser demo — local systems only, no live AI.

2D Tower-Defense × Roguelite Vertical Slice

Scarward (Working Title)

A side-view pixel tower-defense × roguelite set in the world of The Hook and Skein. Hold a Joined-Hands holding-circle near the Scar borderlands against Vrakoni and Hooked Ones, sever handler command-links, and balance Tension and Spool Workings before the Rust takes you.
