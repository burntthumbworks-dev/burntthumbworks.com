Fowl Play

A browser kart racer and battle arena built on public-domain Grimm folklore—solo, split-screen, or P2P online with zero accounts.

Play now Game overview

What it is

Folklore, on wheels.

Fowl Play drops a public-domain Brothers Grimm cast into crooked road races and multi-level arena brawls.

Race Mode

Elimination and Grand Circuit racing across 32 tracks with elevation, drift mini-turbos, and slipstream drafting.

Arena 2.0 Combat

10 multi-level arenas with elevated bridge decks, ramps, and tactical cover—play 3:00 Timed Free-For-All or Stock Survival.

Modular Folk-Toy Garage

Mix riders, chassis, mounts, toppers, and trail FX. Earn local Season 1 XP and tier-up unlocks right in your browser.

Inside the build

Zero install, zero paywalls.

32 Tracks & 10 Arenas

Thirty-two public-domain folklore racers across 32 tracks and 10 Arena 2.0 battlegrounds.

16 Reliquary Tricks

Glass-reliquary pickups with dedicated audio cues, hitmarkers, bumper recoil, and KO streak callouts.

Local & P2P Play

Built on Three.js with local stats saved in your browser and serverless WebRTC room codes for online matches.

Handcrafted games and dependable everyday tools — stewarded by His Grace the Duke of Errol a Dude of Greenthumb Abbey.
