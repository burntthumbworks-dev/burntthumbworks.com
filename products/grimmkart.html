GrimmKart.io

A free browser kart game built on public-domain Grimm folklore. Two modes, 32 characters, last tale standing — no install, no account.

Play free now Game page

What it is

Folklore, on wheels.

GrimmKart takes the cast of the Brothers Grimm — all public-domain — and drops them into a kart game with a wicked streak. Pick a tale, pick a kart, and fight to be the last one standing across two distinct modes.

Race Mode

Elimination racing across 32 cursed circular tracks. Fall to last place and the clock takes you — every 15 seconds the trailing racer is knocked out until one remains.

Battle Arena Mode

Free-drive item combat in 10 contained arenas. Grab glass-reliquary items, use them on your rivals, and survive on stocks. Last fable standing wins.

One shared garage

Both modes share the same 32 characters, 32 karts, 16 items, controls, and stats. Switch modes from the menu — your roster comes with you.

Inside the build

A real game, in your browser.

32 / 32 / 32

Thirty-two folklore characters, thirty-two karts, and thirty-two varied tracks — plus 10 battle arenas.

16 living items

Every glass-reliquary pickup does something you can see and hear — boosts, traps, shields, scrambles, and stranger magic.

Sound & feel

Synthesized audio and particle / screen effects, with a mute toggle. Built on Three.js, no plugins, runs on a phone or a laptop.

Public-domain cast

Original designs rooted in public-domain Grimm tales — no third-party characters, no borrowed brands.

Local stats

Your results are saved in your browser. Online leaderboards are planned, not wired — nothing leaves your device today.

More to come

Rally points, non-circular tracks, and a shrinking cursed-zone battle royale are next on the bench. Follow along in the Lab.
